Licensing

software.pointsav.com distributes pre-compiled binaries under four license tiers, one per product family. All are one-time purchases in native USDC on the Polygon network — there is no subscription, and no recurring charge for continued use of a binary you have already purchased. Every product is priced at $0 during the current BETA period, regardless of which tier applies — tier governs the rights you hold, not what's charged today.

Proprietary

Applies to os-orchestration and its app-orchestration-* surfaces — permanently, no source grant. This is the company's stated commercial moat: a solo Totebox deployment is free forever, and aggregation or resale requires a separate commercial agreement.

FSL-1.1-ALv2 (Functional Source License)

Applies to os-infrastructure, os-network-admin, os-mediakit, os-totebox, and the os-privategit engine (this storefront and its release server). The source is readable, and the license carries one restriction — you may not use the binary or its source to build a product that competes with PointSav. Two years after a given version's release date, that version's license automatically converts to Apache-2.0, at which point the non-compete restriction lapses for that version. Reference: fsl.software.

AGPL-3.0-or-later

Applies to os-console and its app-console-* surfaces — the flagship buy-to-own product line. Source is freely available under AGPL-3.0-or-later's copyleft terms at github.com/pointsav/pointsav-monorepo. A purchase buys the official signed binary and commercial redistribution rights, not access to the code — the binary itself carries no license-key check, no phone-home, and no kill switch, so reading or building the source has never required a purchase.

Apache-2.0 (Open Source)

A genuine, unconditional grant — no purchase, no license key, no BETA gate to lift. Currently applies to tool-wallet, the Polygon USDC watcher this storefront's own payment flow runs on.

What a purchase includes

A purchase is a one-time transaction for the version you buy. There are no subscriptions and no ongoing service commitment — you download the binary, and it is yours to run indefinitely. A license's update-channel window has an expiry date that governs whether that specific purchase still qualifies for newer version downloads; it never blocks or revokes your ability to keep running a version you have already downloaded. Purchasing a later version, when you want one, is a separate transaction at the same tier price.

Source availability

All licensed source code referenced above is published and version-controlled at github.com/pointsav/pointsav-monorepo, satisfying network-service source-availability obligations for any AGPL-licensed components in this stack.
